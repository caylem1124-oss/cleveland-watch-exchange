CLIENT CARE

Your inquiry.
Handled with care.

When you request a quote or callback, CLE Watches receives the contact details, watch interest, message, preferred time and photo/video preferences you submit. We use these details to respond to your inquiry and arrange the next steps you request.

Your shortlist stays in your browser. An inquiry does not reserve a watch, place an order or confirm an appointment.

When you send a quote or callback request, the form prepares a message in WhatsApp on your own device. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp. WhatsApp is operated by Meta, and its terms and privacy policy apply to messages sent through it. Sending an inquiry does not subscribe you to ongoing marketing.

Do not include payment-card details, identity documents or other sensitive information in the form. We retain inquiry information while responding and managing the relationship, and as needed for transaction records or applicable obligations.
